## Core Gameplay Mechanics


The gameplay loop revolves around the "Smash" mechanic. As players take damage, their percentage increases, making them easier to launch into the air or off the stage.


*   **Movement:** Master dash dancing, short-hopping, and air-dodging to navigate the stage effectively.
*   **Combat:** Utilize a mix of standard attacks, tilt attacks, and powerful "Smash" attacks to build damage.
*   **Recovery:** Use character-specific special moves to return to the stage after being knocked away.
*   **Final Smashes:** Unleash cinematic, screen-clearing ultimate moves once the Smash Ball is broken.

## Gameplay Deep Dive: The Spirit System


The Spirit System adds a layer of RPG-like customization to the traditional fighting formula. By collecting spirits—which represent characters from various gaming universes—players can boost their fighter's attack, defense, speed, or gain special resistances. This system is essential for clearing the more difficult challenges in the World of Light mode and allows for creative "builds" that can drastically change how a character performs in battle.

## Tips and Strategy


*   **Master the Recovery:** Every character has a different recovery move. Spend time in training mode learning how to get back to the stage safely.
*   **Don't Just Smash:** While Smash attacks are powerful, they are easily punished if missed. Use your tilt attacks and aerials to control the neutral game.
*   **Shield Management:** Learn to time your shields and parries. A well-timed shield drop can lead to a devastating counter-attack.
*   **Practice Movement:** Movement is just as important as attacking. Practice your movement options to ensure you are never a sitting duck.
